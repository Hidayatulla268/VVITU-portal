System Architecture

End-to-End Operational Pipeline

How requests traverse from client devices through enterprise security layers down to PostgreSQL.

STEP 01
Client Devices
Students, Faculty & HODs access via HTTPS on phones, tablets, or laptops.
STEP 02
WAF & Rate Limiter
Inspects payloads for malicious patterns, applies login rate limits, and injects HSTS.
STEP 03
WhiteNoise Static
Serves compressed CSS, JS, and font assets directly with Brotli/Gzip caching.
STEP 04
Scoped RBAC Router
Enforces role-based permissions (Admin, HOD, Faculty, Student, DEO).
STEP 05
PostgreSQL Storage
Persistent relational database with connection pooling and automated daily backups.
